Градител: играта со слободна камера

This is the same map, the same models and the same rendering as the game. The camera moves freely, so you can walk, fly and look from any angle while you place signs, lights and markings.

Press C to look around. WASD move · Space / Shift up / down · Ctrl fast · Wheel speed · Left click place · Right click remove · 1–9 pick an item · [ ] turn a sign · F select the item you aim at · Del remove selected · X cancel a line · the top-bar mouse button frees or locks the mouse.

Save versions as you go. Export mod writes active.json: copy it to public/mods/active.json and the game applies your signs, lights, lines, zebras, trees and lamps on its next start. Existing signs, lights and markings from the survey file and the map are shown exactly as the game shows them. They are read-only here; you add new ones.
