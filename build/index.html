Копчиња

  • Mouse looks around (click the view first) · Left click place · Right click remove · right-drag looks when the mouse is free
  • 1–9 pick an item · F select the item under the aim · Del remove
  • C mouse look on / free · X cancel a line · Backspace undo the last line point · Del remove selected
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo (10 steps)
